## Goal

Capture and later evaluate a set of animation ideas for Infoschematic rendering and interaction, beyond the existing transient Flow-signal mechanism.

## Context

The only animation in the product today is the transient Flow-signal emphasis (`ADR-INFOSCHEMATICS-012`, `packages/view-canvas/src/styles.css`'s `infoschematic-signal-emphasis` keyframes, and the Flow pulse radius `view-canvas` owns): a deterministic, finite, occurrence-keyed emphasis that never becomes part of the authored, serialisable contract. Further animation ideas exist but have not yet been recorded here — the item is a placeholder to hold them once supplied, rather than a scoped piece of work.

## Boundary
